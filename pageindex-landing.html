The Problem

Why traditional RAG falls short

Vector-based retrieval systems make fundamental assumptions about documents that break down in practice.

  • 🎯

    Similarity ≠ Relevance

    Cosine similarity finds semantically close chunks, not necessarily the most contextually relevant information for your specific query.

  • ✂️

    Chunking destroys context

    Arbitrary text splitting breaks semantic units, separating definitions from their references, tables from their explanations.

  • 🔮

    Black box retrieval

    You cannot explain why a chunk was retrieved or trace the reasoning path from query to answer.

  • 📉

    Long document failure

    Performance degrades significantly with complex 100+ page documents where relationships span sections.

The Approach

A hierarchical tree, not a flat vector space

PageIndex converts your document into a structured semantic tree that mirrors how a human expert navigates a document — by understanding structure, not just similarity.

🌳

Semantic tree structure

Documents are parsed into a hierarchy: document → sections → subsections → pages, preserving all contextual relationships.

🧠

LLM navigates the tree

Instead of similarity scoring, an LLM reasons through the tree structure — like an expert reading a table of contents before diving in.

📍

Precise page selection

The engine identifies the exact pages containing relevant information, with a transparent reasoning trace for every retrieval decision.
